JAPANESE SUPPORT

Japanese Support

So even first-time students can feel at ease, our Japanese-speaking counselors provide consistent support in Japanese from before departure through your return home.

Support Scope

What's included

🇯🇵

Japanese-language counseling

Course selection, pricing, accommodation — every question is handled carefully in Japanese.

📄

Application & document support

Our specialists support you from preparing application documents through submission.

🧳

Pre-departure orientation

What to pack, local life rules, emergency contacts — we cover what you need to know before leaving.

💬

On-the-ground support

After you arrive in Montreal, our local staff remain available for daily life and study questions.

🧒

Minor student support

For students under 18, we coordinate closely with parents to provide extra-attentive support.

🏥

Overseas insurance guidance

We help you understand and arrange overseas insurance in case of sudden illness or trouble abroad.

About minor student support

For middle school and high school students, we offer minor student support in addition to standard support — including a parent contact system and emergency response.
