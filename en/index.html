Why Montreal

The appeal of the bilingual city of Montreal

Montreal is one of North America's most beloved student cities — safe, affordable, and full of European-style charm.

Safe and easy to live in

One of the safer major cities in North America, with reliable public transit — reassuring for first-time students.

Affordable cost of living

More affordable than most major U.S. cities, easing the financial pressure of a longer study abroad stay.

A truly international student city

Home to many universities and language schools, with students from all over the world to meet and connect with.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take both the English Course and the French Course?

To help you focus fully on one language, our courses are kept clearly separate. Please choose the course that matches your current goal.

Are the prices shown official?

The prices currently shown are reference prices set during our pre-opening preparation, based on typical pricing levels among Montreal language schools. They are not DASS's official prices and may change — please confirm before applying.

What's the shortest program length I can apply for?

Programs range from 1 week up to long-term (a year or more). Contact us to discuss the right length for you.
