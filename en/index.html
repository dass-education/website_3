Why Montreal

The appeal of the bilingual city of Montreal

Montreal is one of North America's most beloved student cities — safe, affordable, and full of European-style charm.

More affordable than most major U.S. cities, easing the financial pressure of a longer study abroad stay.

A truly international student city

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take both the English Course and the French Course?

To help you focus fully on one language, our courses are kept clearly separate. Please choose the course that matches your current goal.

I'm a complete beginner in French — is the French Course still for me?

Yes. Many of our French Course students start as complete beginners. Classes are grouped by level, so you can join with confidence.

Do I need any French knowledge for the English Course?

Not at all. The English Course is designed so you can focus entirely on English — no prior French knowledge is required.

What's the shortest program length I can apply for?

Programs range from short-term (2 weeks and up) to long-term (a year or more). Contact us to discuss the right length for you.

How much does it cost?

Costs vary by course, duration, and accommodation type (homestay, student residence, etc). Book a free counseling session and we'll build a plan around your budget.
