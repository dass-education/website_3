ACCOMMODATION

Accommodation

DASS offers two accommodation options — homestay and student residence. Choose the one that fits your lifestyle and goals.

Options

Two accommodation options

HOMESTAY

Homestay

Live with a Canadian or Québécois host family and build language skills and cultural understanding through everyday life. Private rooms are standard, with meal plans available. Ideal for students who want to settle into local life quickly.

STUDENT RESIDENCE

Student Residence

Live alongside students from around the world. Private or shared rooms, with shared kitchens and lounges that naturally spark connections. A great fit for students who want an independent study abroad experience with peers of a similar age.
