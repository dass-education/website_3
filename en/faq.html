FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about courses, pricing, accommodation, and support.

Courses

Can I take both the English Course and the French Course?

To help you focus fully on one language, our courses are kept clearly separate. Please choose the course that matches your current goal.

What's the difference between General and Intensive courses?

General courses run 24 lessons/week (20 hours), while Intensive courses run 30 lessons/week (25 hours). Intensive courses suit students who want faster results in a shorter time.

How long can I take IELTS or DELF/DALF Preparation?

These are typically designed for 4–24 week enrollments. For other durations, please contact us directly.

I'm a complete beginner in French — is the French Course still for me?

Yes. Many of our French Course students start as complete beginners. Classes are grouped by level, so you can join with confidence.

IELTS & DELF/DALF Preparation

Can beginners take IELTS Preparation?

IELTS Preparation is recommended for intermediate level and above. Beginners are encouraged to build foundational skills first with General or Intensive English. Please check the level guidance in a free consultation.

Is the IELTS exam fee included in tuition?

No. The IELTS exam fee is not included in DASS tuition — it is paid separately to the exam administrator.

Is my target IELTS score guaranteed?

No. Taking this course does not guarantee achievement of any target score. Progress varies by student.

What's the difference between DELF and DALF?

Both are French-language proficiency qualifications. DELF covers levels A1, A2, B1, and B2; DALF covers C1 and C2. Preparation content depends on your target level.

Which levels does DELF/DALF Preparation cover?

Which exam levels are offered depends on the time of year and the number of interested students. If you have a specific level in mind, please contact us.

Is the DELF/DALF exam fee included?

No. DELF/DALF exam fees are not included in DASS tuition — they are paid separately to the exam administrator.

Can Exam Preparation courses start any week?

Unlike General and Intensive courses, Exam Preparation start dates are set dates or depend on availability — not necessarily every Monday. Please contact us in advance to confirm.

Can I take Exam Preparation on its own?

Yes — IELTS Preparation and DELF/DALF Preparation are independent courses you can take on their own. They typically start from 4 weeks, and a minimum enrollment number or other conditions may apply. Please contact us for details.

Tuition & Fees

Are the prices shown official?

The prices currently shown are reference prices set during our pre-opening preparation, based on typical pricing levels among Montreal language schools. They are not DASS's official prices and may change — please confirm before applying.

Are there costs beyond course tuition?

Yes — registration, materials, accommodation arrangement, airport pickup, and overseas insurance are all separate. See the Tuition & Fees page for details.

Where can I get an official quote?

The "Get a Quote" page currently shows an estimate preview only. For an official quote, please book a free consultation with our Japanese-speaking counselors.

Accommodation

Homestay or student residence — which do you recommend?

Homestay suits students who want full cultural immersion; student residence suits students who want to connect freely with peers. See the Accommodation page for details.

Is airport pickup available to my homestay or residence?

Yes — one-way and round-trip airport pickup is available (reference price: CAD 135 one-way, CAD 270 round-trip). Let us know at the time of application if you'd like this arranged.

Support & Admissions

What's the shortest program I can apply for?

Programs are available from 1 week up to long-term (a year or more). Contact us to discuss the right length for you.

Can minors (middle/high school students) apply?

Yes, with parental consent, as part of a plan that includes minor student support. See the Japanese Support page for details.

Is it okay to come alone?

Many students study abroad solo. Between orientation from local staff and opportunities to meet other students, you'll settle in quickly.
