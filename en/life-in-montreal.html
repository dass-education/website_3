LIFE IN MONTREAL

Life in Montréal

One of North America's most beloved student cities, with European-style charm and a safe, livable environment. Here's what makes Montreal special as a study destination.

Safe and easy to live in

One of the safer major cities in North America, with reliable public transit — reassuring for first-time students.

Affordable cost of living

More affordable than most major U.S. cities, easing the financial pressure of a longer study abroad stay.

A truly international student city

Home to many universities and language schools, with students from all over the world to meet and connect with.

Bilingual City

A bilingual city where both English and French work

Montreal, in Québec, is North America's only major city with French as its official language — yet English is also widely used. Whether you choose the English Course or the French Course, everyday life rarely poses a language barrier.

  • Well-developed public transit (metro and bus) makes getting around easy
  • Distinct seasons — summer festivals, winter sports, and everything in between
  • Plenty of cafés and restaurants in popular student neighborhoods
  • Universities and language schools bring students from around the world
Culture

French-speaking culture, woven into daily life

Québec's distinctive culture is part of the fun of studying here — for French Course students, of course, but for English Course students too.

Café culture

Distinctly French-speaking cafés line the streets, offering everyday brushes with a different culture.

Festivals year-round

Music festivals and other Québec-specific cultural events happen throughout the year.

Cobblestone old town

Wander cobblestone streets reminiscent of Europe on your days off.

Have questions about life in Montreal?

From weather to safety to cost of living, our Japanese-speaking counselors are happy to help before you go.
