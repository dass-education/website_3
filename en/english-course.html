IELTS Preparation in Detail

Inside the IELTS Preparation course

Covers all four IELTS skills — Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking — building step by step from understanding question formats to practical exam strategy.

🎧

Listening

Training in question formats and time management for the Listening section.

📖

Reading

Practice with answering techniques and time management for each question type.

✍️

Writing

Structure and language for Writing Task 1 (describing graphs/diagrams) and Task 2 (essay).

🗣️

Speaking

Repeated practice through mock Speaking tests modeled on the real exam format.

What the course covers

  • Understanding question formats across Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking
  • Practical exercises focused on time management
  • Writing Task 1 and Task 2 structure and language practice
  • Mock Speaking tests
  • Practice with sample questions
  • Individual feedback from instructors
  • Vocabulary and grammar reinforcement
Please note — IELTS Preparation
· The IELTS exam fee is not included in DASS tuition. It is paid separately to the exam administrator.
· This course provides IELTS exam preparation lessons; DASS does not administer the IELTS exam itself.
· Please confirm exam venues, dates, and registration procedures directly with the official IELTS exam administrator.
· Taking this course does not guarantee achievement of any target score.
· A minimum enrollment number or other conditions may apply before the course can run.

FAQ

Common questions, answered

I'm not confident in my English — will I be okay?

Don't worry. An entry-level check places you in a class that matches your current level, and beginner-friendly classes are well supported.

Can I really get by on English alone in a French-speaking city?

Montreal is a bilingual city where English is widely used. School life and daily life work perfectly well in English alone.

Should I choose General English or Intensive English?

General English suits students who want balanced overall growth; Intensive English suits those who want faster results in a shorter time. Feel free to ask us if you're unsure.
