🗣️

Focus on practical conversation

🌎

A multinational classroom

Studying alongside students from around the world naturally builds your English communication skills.

Pathway & exam-prep options

Curriculum

FAQ

Answers to common concerns

I'm not confident in my English — will I be okay?

Don't worry. A placement test at the start of the program places you in the class that matches your level, including plenty of beginner-friendly options.

Montreal is in a French-speaking region — can I really get by with just English?

Yes. Montreal is a bilingual city where English is widely used. You can manage your studies and daily life entirely in English.

I'm worried about coming alone — is that common?

Many of our students come alone. With staff-led orientation and student mixer events, you'll make friends quickly.
