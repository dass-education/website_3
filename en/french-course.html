DELF/DALF Preparation in Detail

Inside the DELF/DALF Preparation course

Covers all four skills — Compréhension de l'oral (listening), Compréhension des écrits (reading), Production écrite (writing), and Production orale (speaking) — building step by step from understanding exam format to practical exam strategy.

🎧

Compréhension de l'oral

Training in question formats and time management for the listening section.

📖

Compréhension des écrits

Reading comprehension techniques and time management for each question type.

✍️

Production écrite

Writing practice modeled on the exam format, with instructor correction.

🗣️

Production orale

Repeated practice through mock oral exams modeled on the real format.

What the course covers

  • Understanding exam format across all four skills (listening, reading, writing, speaking)
  • Practical exercises focused on time management
  • Practice with sample questions
  • Oral exam (Production orale) practice
  • Correction of written work (Production écrite)
  • Level-specific preparation (A1–C2)
About DELF/DALF levels
DELF covers levels A1, A2, B1, and B2; DALF covers C1 and C2. Which exam levels are offered depends on the time of year and the number of interested students.
Please note — DELF/DALF Preparation
· DELF/DALF exam fees are not included in DASS tuition. They are paid separately to the exam administrator.
· This course provides DELF/DALF exam preparation lessons; DASS is not an official exam center.
· Please confirm official exam dates, venues, and registration procedures directly with the exam administrator.
· Taking this course does not guarantee a passing result.
· A minimum enrollment number or other conditions may apply before the course can run.

FAQ

Common questions, answered

I'm a complete beginner — is the French Course still for me?

Yes. Many of our French Course students start as complete beginners. Classes are grouped by level, so you can join with confidence.

Should I choose General French or Intensive French?

General French suits students who want balanced overall growth; Intensive French suits those who want faster results in a shorter time.

What level do I need to start DELF/DALF Preparation?

Based on your entry-level check, we support levels from A1 (beginner) through C2 (most advanced), matched to your target certification.
