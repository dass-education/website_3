Montreal is a city where French is the official language. Learn authentic French — and its culture — in Canada's safe, welcoming environment.

Course Features

Features of the French Course

A program built for French learners, from complete beginners to experienced speakers.

📚

Beginner-friendly, level-based classes

Even with zero French experience, you'll start with careful, foundational instruction.

🗣️

Practical French conversation

What you learn in class, you can put to use right away at a nearby café or shop.

🎭

Classes rooted in Québec culture

Beyond language, you'll learn about the distinctive history, culture, and customs of Québec.

🏠

French-speaking homestays

We place you with French-speaking host families, extending your learning into daily life.

🎓

DELF/DALF exam preparation available

Dedicated prep classes are available for students pursuing the DELF or DALF French certifications.

🇯🇵

Support from Japanese counselors

Even as a French beginner, you can prepare with confidence with Japanese-language support.

Curriculum

Why Montreal

Culture

Experiencing French-speaking culture in North America

Québec, home to Montreal, is the only officially French-speaking region in North America. From cobblestone old-town streets to café culture and French signage, French-speaking charm is woven into everyday life here.

Cobblestone streets in the old town

Wander streets that feel like Europe, surrounded by French signage and storefronts.

Everyday café culture

Enjoy authentic French-speaking café culture and hear living, spoken French every day.

Year-round festivals and events

From music festivals to cultural events, Québec hosts a rich calendar of celebrations all year long.
