ADMISSIONS

Admissions

From your first inquiry to departure, the DASS team supports you at every step.

Step by Step

The application process

1

Free counseling

We'll learn about your goals and recommend the right course.

2

Course & accommodation selection

We introduce course options and accommodation plans (homestay, student residence) that match your needs.

3

Application & payment

Submit your application documents and complete payment for registration and course fees.

4

Pre-departure orientation

We walk you through what to pack and what to expect, so you can leave with confidence.

5

Arrival & local support

After you arrive in Montreal, our local staff continues to support your daily life and studies.

Documents

Typical information needed to apply

Required documents vary by course and duration. We'll walk you through the details during your free counseling session.

📇

Passport (photo page showing name and date of birth)

📝

Application form and consent form

🏠

Your accommodation type and duration preferences

👪

For minors: parental consent and contact information

Minors

Applying as a minor

Applications from middle school and high school students are accepted with parental consent, as part of a plan that includes minor student support (reference fee: CAD 150). See the Japanese Support page for details.

Please note
Class hours, lesson counts, start dates, and class sizes are subject to change. Please confirm official details before enrolling. The prices shown are reference prices during pre-opening preparation and are not DASS's official prices.
