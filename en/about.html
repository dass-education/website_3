ABOUT DASS

About DASS

DASS Montréal Language School is built around letting students focus fully on either English or French, backed by thorough Japanese-language support.

Our Philosophy

The value of focusing on one language

Trying to learn English and French at the same time can leave both half-finished. At DASS, you first choose the English Course or the French Course based on your goal, then focus fully on that one language throughout the program.

While still enjoying the bilingual city of Montreal as your backdrop, narrowing your study to a single language means you can see real progress, even in a short program.
